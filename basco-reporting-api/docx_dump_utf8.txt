CONTENT & UX PRINCIPLES
The landing and login experience should remain intentionally light on copy. The objective is not to explain the entire BASCO program before users enter the platform, but to establish the value of the experience and create confidence in the intelligence available behind the login.
Landing Page
The page should communicate three things quickly:
What is this?A retail marketing intelligence experience powered by BASCO.
Why should I care?It provides visibility into retailer execution and helps identify where attention or action is needed.
What should I do next?Access my retailer insights.
The dashboard preview should therefore carry much of the storytelling visually.
Login Page
The login experience should feel like a continuation of the BASCO platform rather than a separate IT authentication screen.
Keep authentication simple and prominent, with supporting content limited to:
What the user is accessing
How they sign in
Why their view may be personalized
Where they can get help
POSITIONING PRINCIPLE
The external-facing experience should position BASCO beyond reporting or compliance monitoring.
The intended progression is:
Retail execution  Intelligence  Decisions  Action
The platform should therefore consistently feel like a source of retail marketing intelligence and decision support, rather than simply a repository of dashboards and data.
